Beyond Skyrim - Bruma 2 - Child Clothing Fix: third-party material

The plugin overrides one quest record of Beyond Skyrim - Bruma (Nexus Mods 10917, by The Beyond Skyrim - Cyrodiil
Development Team) and copies that record's data with two properties filled in. It stays under
the Beyond Skyrim team's permissions.

Credit goes to the authors of the mods below. Permission status is based on each mod's Nexus page permissions, read on 2026-11-13.

- Beyond Skyrim - Bruma SE (Nexus Mods 10917) by The Beyond Skyrim - Cyrodiil Development Team: allowed based on the Nexus page permissions
- Beyond Skyrim - Assets SE (Nexus Mods 198585) by BeyondSkyrimDC: allowed based on the Nexus page permissions

Share fixes with the Beyond Skyrim team.

Requires Beyond Skyrim - Bruma 2.0.0 (Nexus 10917) and Beyond Skyrim - Assets SE (Nexus 198585).
